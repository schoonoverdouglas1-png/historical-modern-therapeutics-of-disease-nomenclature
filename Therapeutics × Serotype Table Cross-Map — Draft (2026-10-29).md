**Reading the gaps honestly:** of these 16 rows, 11 carry GAP in both the fungal and yeast columns, and 3 are GAP in all three columns (Meningococcal disease, Typhus, Poliomyelitis), with Rabies and Tetanus also naming no bacterial co-pathogen — the map does not fill those gaps. The fungal column is populated for only 5 of the 16 (Tuberculosis, Scrofula-note, Malaria-partial, Influenza, Dengue-exceptional), and 2 of those 5 are partial/exceptional only.
